How to play

  1. Each team gets its own question. Answer on your own keypad.
  2. Tap the numbers, then press ✓ to submit. Press ✕ to clear.
  3. Correct answer: your team pulls the rope one step and scores a point.
  4. Wrong answer: the other team scores the point and pulls! Think before you press ✓.
  5. Be the first to lead by 6 points and pull the other team across the dashed line to win!
